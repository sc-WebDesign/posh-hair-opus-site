## Why these two

**The hero is the job she is actually known for.** Blonde work coming up through dark hair is the
going-back-to-my-natural-colour story her strongest review tells. Corrective colour is the
highest-anxiety and highest-value work in hairdressing, it is what people search for at eleven at
night after a box dye has gone wrong, and it is currently impossible to find her while doing it.

**The second frame is as close as stock comes to a strand test**, which is this build's whole spine:
two hands isolating and clipping a single section. A strand test is the least glamorous ten minutes in
a salon and the one professional habit that prevents colour disasters. It is invisible when it works,
which is why almost nobody writes about it, and why several clients writing about it here matters.

## Two rejections worth keeping

**A busy salon floor.** One technically clean colour frame showed several stylists working at once in
the background. **Posh Hair by Ada is one person in one room on Philip Lane.** A six-chair floor says
chain, or training school, and quietly contradicts the thing the reviews are actually about, which is
one person paying attention to you. **Check the size of the place in the background, not just the
trade.**

**The same photograph twice.** `29555677` is the same client, same towel, same session as the hero and
almost the same distance. Both are good; used together they would have read as one picture printed
twice. The fix was not a different shoot but a different distance, which is why the pair here is one
wide and one tight.

## A note on gloves, for this trade specifically

Elsewhere in this programme disposable gloves have been a reason to reject a frame, because latex and
hairnets read clinical in businesses selling warmth. **Hair colour is different.** Gloves there are
trade-correct and read professional, the same way a chef's whites do. A colour frame should never be
rejected for gloves alone.

Separately: the stock descriptions claimed gloves on **both** accepted frames and both show bare hands
throughout. Read the descriptions to decide what to download, never to decide what to use.
